Fixed settings and changes

History length is a number of observations, not frames. The validity period is counted in native frames and also applies to each observation in the history. Thresholds and candidate margins are in fixed-area OKS similarity units. G/SP/S1/S2 use only past history; P0/P1 use both past and future. The input context is the same. Optical flow, Kalman prediction and forward-backward agreement were not added in this run. S2→SP compares the nodes used; SP→G0 is a comparison that includes several design changes.

Why the parameters matter

In G0 the unmatched threshold 0.05 is also counted among the second-best options, so a committed link that satisfies the 0.05 candidate margin needs a similarity of at least 0.10. Of the 284 insufficient-margin cases in the main 20 s, 273 were below this effective floor. Adjusting the candidate margin affects both how competition is handled and the minimum similarity for a commit.

Similarity is computed as the mean OKS against each past observed position. At side1 f75286, even though the preceding move was 4.64 px and the other detection was 317 px away, widening the history from 5 to 9 observations took the similarity 0.166→0.092, below the commit condition. This example was confirmed by independently recomputing the effect of past positions receding.

History 3 observations and candidate margin 0.02, where a tuning effect was visible, are separate conditions, not the result of applying both at once. Design and verification evidence

SLEAP's per-track histories are used, and a history is updated once even on an ambiguous provisional assignment. When the candidate margin is insufficient only the output fragment is cut, and the fragment ID is not used as the key of a new competing history. There is also no step that discounts the uncertainty of old predictions to favour them.

At the 3D entry a negative label leads to the detection being excluded, so every observation is given a nonnegative ID, singleton fragments included. The global row and the within-frame source offset are recorded separately. This run's interval outputs do not replace the production whole-recording C27.
